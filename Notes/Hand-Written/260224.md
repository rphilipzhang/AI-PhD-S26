TD: Bellman operator + bootstrap sampling

TD-based control method: SARSA, Q-learning

SARSA: Q(s,a)<-Q(s,a)+alpha*(r_t+gamma * Q(s_t+1,a_t+1)-Q(s_t,a_t)), wherea a_t and, s_t+1 a_t+1 ~ pi; do epsilon-greedy to update pi

SARSA is an on-policy learning technique

Q-learning: Q(s,a)<-Q(s,a)+alpha*(r_t+gamma * max_a Q(s_t+1,a)-Q(s_t,a_t)), wherea a_t and, s_t+1 ~ pi, but  a_t+1 is assumed to be the greedy policy in period t+1; do epsilon-greedy to update pi

Q-learning is an off-policy leanring technique; epsilon-greedy as the behavior policy, and greedy as the target policy.
